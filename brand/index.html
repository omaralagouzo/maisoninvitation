Usage

  • Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the “A”.
  • Don’t stretch, outline, add shadows, or re-type the wordmark in another font.
  • On dark backgrounds or photos use the reversed or mono-white versions.
  • Minimum widths: lockup 120px · wordmark 90px · mark 16px.
